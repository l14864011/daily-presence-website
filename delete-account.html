Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Presence account and all associated data at any time, using either of the methods below.

Option 1 — Delete from the app

In-app account deletion (instant)

Open Presence → tap your profile icon → Account → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete Account. You will be asked to re-authenticate before deletion is confirmed.

Option 2 — Submit a request by email

Email us

Send a deletion request from the email address associated with your account to:

lxl640213@gmail.com

Please include Account Deletion Request in the subject line. We will process your request within 7 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account credentials (email, name)
  • All session history and guidance records
  • Voice and language preferences
  • Any photos you submitted for guidance generation

What is retained

Anonymized, non-identifiable usage statistics may be retained for up to 90 days for internal analytics, after which they are permanently deleted.

For questions about your data, see our Privacy Policy or contact us at lxl640213@gmail.com.
