What is Presence

A mindful guide for your real moment, in real time.

Tell it what you're doing, how you feel, or share a photo, and it creates a short spoken guidance for that exact moment — grounded in your senses, your surroundings, and the present.

1

Share your moment

Say what you're doing — or add a photo of where you are.

2

Breathe & receive

A spoken guidance unfolds around a calm breathing circle — about two minutes.

3

Keep a moment card

A reflective line from your session — yours to keep, or share with someone.
